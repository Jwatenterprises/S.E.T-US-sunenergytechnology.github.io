Data Deletion

How to request removal of your personal data from S.E.T. — Sun Energy Technology.

Effective date: August 6, 2026
Overview

Request Data Deletion

You have the right to request deletion of any personal data we hold about you. This includes your email address, name, newsletter subscription records, and any other personally identifiable information associated with your account or interactions with our website.

We take data deletion requests seriously and will process your request within 30 days of receipt.

How To

How to Request Deletion

1

Send an Email

Email us at biz@sunenergytechnology.net with the subject line "Data Deletion Request". Include the email address associated with your data so we can locate your records.

2

We Verify Your Identity

To protect your privacy, we may ask you to confirm your identity before processing the deletion. This is typically done by replying from the email address on file.

3

We Delete Your Data

Once verified, we will delete your personal data from our systems within 30 days. You will receive a confirmation email when the deletion is complete.

✉ Send Deletion Request
What Gets Deleted

Data We Will Remove

Upon a valid deletion request, we will remove:

  • Your email address from our newsletter and mailing lists (Brevo)
  • Any contact form submissions or messages you sent us
  • Your name and any profile information associated with your email
  • Quiz or calculator data linked to your account (if applicable)

Data We Cannot Delete

Some data is controlled by third parties and must be deleted through their platforms:

  • Facebook/Meta — manage your data at Facebook Settings
  • Google Analytics — anonymized/aggregated analytics data cannot be traced back to individuals
  • Affiliate networks (Awin, Amazon, CJ) — these track purchase activity under their own privacy policies
  • YouTube — manage your data at Google Account Settings

Facebook Login Users

If you connected to our site via Facebook Login (Meta), you can also remove our app's access to your data by going to Facebook → Settings → Apps and Websites, finding "S.E.T. Solar," and clicking "Remove." This will trigger automatic deletion of any data we received through Facebook.

Timeline

Processing Timeline

  • Acknowledgment: Within 3 business days of receiving your request
  • Verification: 1–5 business days (if needed)
  • Deletion complete: Within 30 calendar days of verified request
  • Confirmation: Email notification when deletion is complete

Questions?
