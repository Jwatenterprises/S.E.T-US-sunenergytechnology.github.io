Privacy Policy

How S.E.T. — Sun Energy Technology collects, uses, and protects your information.

Effective date: August 6, 2026 • Last updated: August 6, 2026
Overview

Who We Are

S.E.T. — Sun Energy Technology (sunenergytechnology.com) is a solar energy education and product review website operated by Jwat Enterprise Inc. We provide independent reviews, comparisons, and guides for solar generators, panels, batteries, and related equipment.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our practices.

Information We Collect

What Data We Gather

Information You Provide

We may collect information you voluntarily provide, including:

  • Contact information — your name and email address when you subscribe to our newsletter, fill out a contact form, or reach out via email
  • Quiz and calculator inputs — the answers you enter into our solar sizing quiz, savings calculator, or comparison tools (these are processed locally in your browser and are not stored on our servers)
  • Comments and feedback — any messages or feedback you send us directly

Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our site, certain information is collected automatically through cookies and similar technologies:

  • Usage data — pages visited, time on site, click patterns, referring URL, and browser type
  • Device information — operating system, screen resolution, and device type (desktop, mobile, tablet)
  • IP address — used for approximate geographic location (country/region level) and security
  • Cookies and pixels — we use Meta Pixel, Google Analytics, and affiliate tracking cookies (see "Third-Party Services" below)

Usage

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Deliver and improve our website content, reviews, and tools
  • Send you newsletters or updates you subscribed to (you can unsubscribe at any time)
  • Track affiliate referrals and commissions through our partner programs
  • Understand how visitors use our site so we can create better content
  • Prevent fraud and ensure website security
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We do not use your data for purposes unrelated to operating this website.

Third Parties

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may collect data when you visit our site:

  • Google Analytics — website traffic analysis. Google's Privacy Policy
  • Meta Pixel (Facebook) — measures ad effectiveness and website engagement. Meta's Privacy Policy
  • Awin, Amazon Associates, CJ Affiliate, Impact — affiliate tracking cookies that record when you click an affiliate link and make a purchase. These cookies are placed by the respective affiliate network, not by us
  • YouTube (embedded videos) — may set cookies when you play embedded videos. Google/YouTube Privacy Policy
  • Brevo (email) — processes newsletter subscriptions and sends emails on our behalf. Brevo's Privacy Policy

Each third-party service operates under its own privacy policy. We encourage you to review their policies for details on how they handle your data.

Cookies

Cookies & Tracking

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. We use them for:

  • Essential cookies — required for the website to function (e.g., remembering quiz progress)
  • Analytics cookies — help us understand traffic patterns (Google Analytics)
  • Advertising cookies — used by Meta Pixel to measure campaign performance
  • Affiliate cookies — track referrals so we receive credit for purchases made through our links

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies. Note that disabling cookies may affect website functionality.

Your Rights

Your Data Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction — ask us to correct inaccurate data
  • Deletion — request that we delete your personal data (see our Data Deletion page)
  • Opt-out — unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time via the link in every email
  • Portability — request your data in a portable format
  • Objection — object to processing of your data for certain purposes

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at biz@sunenergytechnology.net. We will respond within 30 days.

California Residents (CCPA)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act, you have the right to know what personal information we collect, request its deletion, and opt out of the sale of personal information. We do not sell personal information. To make a CCPA request, email biz@sunenergytechnology.net with "CCPA Request" in the subject line.

Data Security

How We Protect Your Information

Our website is served over HTTPS (encrypted connection). We use GitHub Pages for hosting, which provides infrastructure-level security. We limit access to any personal data to only those who need it to operate the website.

While we take reasonable measures to protect your information, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us at biz@sunenergytechnology.net and we will promptly delete it.

Changes

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
