Spec Translator

Confused by Specs? We Translate the Jargon.

Solar generator specs can be overwhelming. Here are the 3 most important ones — in plain English.

2000Wh Capacity
= Powers your fridge for ~13 hours
Watt-hours (Wh) = total energy stored. A 150W refrigerator running for 13 hours = ~2,000Wh consumed. That’s your baseline target for emergency prep.
2000W AC Output
= Runs most home appliances simultaneously
AC output (W) = how many things you can power at once. A 2,000W output handles a refrigerator, lights, phone chargers, and a fan all running at the same time.
LFP Battery Chemistry
= 3,500+ charge cycles vs. 500 for cheaper alternatives
Lithium Iron Phosphate (LFP) batteries last 7x longer than standard lithium-ion. That’s 10+ years of daily use. For any serious investment, LFP is the only battery chemistry worth buying.
