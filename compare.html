Matchup #1
Jackery Explorer 2000 Plus vs EcoFlow Delta 2 Max
Both are 2,000Wh LFP powerhouses, but the Delta 2 Max edges ahead with faster AC recharge (1 hour vs. 2 hours) and higher surge output. Jackery wins on portability and easier solar expansion. For emergency backup: EcoFlow. For camping: Jackery.
Winner: EcoFlow Delta 2 Max (emergency) | Jackery 2000 Plus (camping)

Matchup #2
EcoFlow Delta Pro vs Jackery Explorer 2000 Plus
The Delta Pro is in a different class — nearly double the capacity (3,600 vs 2,042Wh) and expandable to 25kWh. However, the Jackery 2000 Plus costs $1,000–$1,500 less and handles most emergency scenarios at a fraction of the price. Budget drives this decision.
Winner: EcoFlow Delta Pro (off-grid) | Jackery 2000 Plus (value)

Matchup #3
Anker SOLIX C1000 vs EcoFlow River 3 Plus
The SOLIX C1000 offers significantly more capacity (1,056 vs 286Wh) and higher AC output for only about $500 more. If you need to run any real appliances, the River 3 Plus will run out fast. The SOLIX wins on power-to-dollar ratio above $400 budgets.
Winner: Anker SOLIX C1000 (power) | EcoFlow River 3 Plus (ultra-budget)

4Patriots AlphaCase Elite vs Jackery Explorer 2000 Plus

Matchup #7 — Home Backup Heavyweights
Mango Power E vs EcoFlow Delta Pro
Both sit near $3,500 with ~3,500Wh LFP capacity and 3,000W AC output — but they compete differently. If you want the best-established home backup system: EcoFlow. If you want more power per dollar from a newer brand: Mango Power E.
