How many devices do you need to power?

Think about what you’d run during a typical outage or trip — not the absolute maximum.

📱 Just the Essentials Phones, lights, maybe a fan (1–3 devices)
💻 Small Setup Laptop, fridge, lights, devices (4–6)
🏠 Full Household Fridge, TV, CPAP, multiple devices (7–10)
⚡ Everything Run the whole house including heavy appliances (10+)

What’s your budget for a solar generator?

Including any solar panels you’ll buy to charge it. Be honest — better to know your real budget upfront.

💙 Under $500 Tight budget, need to get started
💚 $500–$1,500 Mid-range, most popular budget
💛 $1,500–$3,000 Premium range, serious capability
⭐ $3,000+ Maximum capability, no compromises
Step 4 of 5

How important is portability to you?

Heavier units typically offer more power. Lighter units sacrifice capacity for mobility.

🏃 Very Important Must be lightweight & easy to carry (<30 lbs)
🛍 Somewhat Important Can handle moderate weight with wheels
🏠 Not Important Staying in one place, want maximum power
