How to Choose

  1. Add up your daily watt-hours. A small RV or cabin often uses 1,000–3,000Wh a day. Essentials during a home outage (fridge, lights, router, phones) are usually 2,000–5,000Wh.
  2. Pick voltage. 12V for RVs, vans, boats and small solar kits. 48V for home solar with a 48V inverter.
  3. Check the climate. If it will charge below 32°F, choose self-heating or keep the battery indoors.
  4. Match the BMS to your inverter. A 100A BMS supports about 1,200W continuous. For a 2,000W inverter, use the 200Ah Plus (200A BMS) or two batteries in parallel.
  5. Set your charger to LiFePO4. Never use lead-acid or equalize settings on lithium.
💡 Our take: Redodo and LiTime are the two budget LiFePO4 brands we recommend most. Compare both on the day you buy and take whichever is cheaper for the size you need. If you want an 11-year warranty and the best cold-weather discharge rating, pay more for Dakota Lithium. If you want a plug-and-play box with outlets built in, you need a portable power station instead.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which Redodo battery should I buy for solar?

For most small systems, the 12V 100Ah ($269.99 on Nov 2, 2026): 1,280Wh, 25 lbs, 4,000+ cycles at 100% depth of discharge and a 5-year warranty. For a bigger 12V bank, the 12V 300Ah ($599.99) is the lowest cost per kWh in the lineup. For 48V home solar, the 51.2V 100Ah ($929.99) holds 5,120Wh in one unit.

Is Redodo a good LiFePO4 brand?

Redodo has over 10 years of LiFePO4 experience and says it has sold over 1 million batteries. Its batteries use LiFePO4 cells with a built-in BMS, and the 12V 100Ah and 300Ah listings carry a 5-year warranty. It sits in the same budget tier as LiTime: much cheaper than premium brands, with a shorter warranty.

Do I need a self-heating battery?

Only if the battery will be charged below freezing. LiFePO4 cells should not be charged below 32°F. The Redodo 12V 100Ah Self-Heating uses a 100W heater that turns on automatically below 32°F so it can keep charging from solar in the cold. In mild climates a standard model is fine.

Can I connect Redodo batteries together?

Yes. Same model, same age: parallel adds capacity at the same voltage, series raises voltage. Redodo lists the 12V 300Ah for up to 4P4S, and the 48V 100Ah for up to four in parallel (20.48kWh).

Are Redodo prices always this low?

No. Redodo runs frequent sales, and the prices on this page are the sale prices on its US site on November 2, 2026. Check the live price before you buy.
