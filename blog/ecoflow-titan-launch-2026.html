EcoFlow TITAN Is Here:
Early Bird Deal, Specs & Who It’s For

EcoFlow just launched TITAN, a 5kWh whole-home battery that scales to 30kWh. From October 13 through November 2, a $50 early bird coupon locks in 5% off plus two free 220W solar panels. Here’s what it is, what it costs, and whether it fits your home.

By S.E.T. Editorial Team Published October 13, 2026 🕑 6 min read

Quick Answer: EcoFlow TITAN is a whole-home backup battery: 5kWh per unit, expandable to 30kWh, in three models (5000 Plus and 5000 at 7.2kW and 120/240V; 5000 Classic at 4kW and 120V only). It launched October 13, 2026. Final prices aren’t posted yet; EcoFlow puts the 5000 and 5000 Plus in the $3,000 range and the Classic in the $2,000 range before installation. Through November 2, a $50 early bird coupon secures 5% off plus two free 220W solar panels, and EcoFlow lists $300 off its installation service. It’s best for homeowners who want permanent, panel-integrated backup without paying Powerwall-style installed prices.

EcoFlow TITAN: What You Need to Know

EcoFlow TITAN 5000 Plus. Image: EcoFlow.

TITAN is EcoFlow’s new whole-home backup series. Unlike the DELTA line, which started as portable power stations that can also back up a house, TITAN is built to sit permanently next to your electrical panel. Each unit holds a 5kWh LFP battery (5,017.6Wh). You can add TITAN Batteries in 5kWh steps up to 30kWh. Pair it with EcoFlow’s Smart Home Panel 10 and it covers up to 10 circuits at 50A. EcoFlow says it switches over in 20 milliseconds or less when the grid drops.

There are three models. The TITAN 5000 Plus is the flagship: 7.2kW output, 120/240V, and 8kW of solar input across two MPPTs. EcoFlow says output can reach 10kW when solar is feeding the system. The TITAN 5000 has the same 7.2kW and 240V capability with half the solar input (4kW). The TITAN 5000 Classic is the budget model: 4kW output, 120V only, for homes that only need essentials covered.

In EcoFlow’s lineup, TITAN sits beside the DELTA Pro Ultra as a second whole-home option. It has a lower starting capacity and expected price, and it’s designed for installation rather than portability.

Who Is TITAN For?

TITAN 5000 Plus is for you if you have (or plan to add) a large rooftop solar array, you need 240V for central AC, a well pump, or an electric dryer, and you want room to grow toward 30kWh.

TITAN 5000 is for you if you need 240V whole-home backup but have a smaller solar array (or none). It keeps the 7.2kW output at a lower expected price.

TITAN 5000 Classic is for you if you only need to cover 120V essentials during outages: fridge, lights, Wi-Fi, furnace blower, and a microwave. It does not run 240V appliances.

Skip TITAN if you want something you can take camping or move between locations. At 110–121 lb and built for panel integration, it isn’t portable. A DELTA 2 Max or DELTA Pro is the better fit there. Also skip it if you need to buy today: final pricing isn’t out yet.
