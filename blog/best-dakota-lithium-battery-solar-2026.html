How to Charge Dakota Lithium with Solar

Charging a Dakota Lithium battery with solar is straightforward, but you need the right components. Here’s the basic setup:

  1. Solar panels — BougeRV 200W portable or rigid panels for permanent mount. 200W charges a 100Ah battery from 20% to 100% in 5–6 hours of good sun.
  2. MPPT charge controller — BougeRV Sunflow 30A ($89) or Victron SmartSolar. Must be set to LiFePO4 profile (14.4V absorption, 13.6V float). Never use a lead-acid charging profile on lithium batteries.
  3. Wiring — 10AWG minimum for runs under 20ft. MC4 from panels to controller, ring terminals from controller to battery.
  4. Inverter (optional) — to convert 12V DC to 110V AC for household appliances. Size to your load — a 1,000W pure sine wave inverter covers most essentials.

Sizing rule: Panel watts ÷ peak sun hours = daily recharge capacity. In most of the U.S., expect 4–6 peak sun hours per day. So 200W of panels × 5 hours = 1,000Wh of daily recharge — covering about 78% of a fully depleted 100Ah battery per day.

Can I connect multiple batteries together?

Yes. Parallel for same voltage + combined capacity (e.g., two 12V 100Ah = 12V 200Ah). Series for higher voltage (e.g., two 12V = 24V). Use same model and age. Built-in BMS supports both configurations.
