Jackery Explorer 2000 Plus vs EcoFlow DELTA 2 Max: Power vs Portability

Two of the most popular 2kWh LiFePO4 power stations, compared on the specs that matter: output, solar input, weight, expansion, and today’s price.

S.E.T. Editorial Updated: November 10, 2026 Read: 9 min

Quick Answer: The Jackery Explorer 2000 Plus is the more powerful unit: 3,000W continuous output (6,000W surge) vs 2,400W, up to 1,400W of solar input vs 1,000W, and expansion to 12kWh vs 6,144Wh. The EcoFlow DELTA 2 Max is about 11 lbs lighter (50 lbs vs 61.5 lbs), charges from the wall at up to 1,800W, and is the easier buy right now at $1,029 (EcoFlow, Nov 10, 2026). Choose Jackery for maximum output and expansion; choose EcoFlow for lighter weight and value.

Correction & lineup update (Nov 10, 2026): An earlier version of this comparison listed the wrong Jackery specs (2,200W output, 500W solar input, 50.7 lbs) and swapped the two units’ weights. The specs below come from the manufacturers’ published spec pages. Also new: Jackery’s U.S. store now sells the Explorer 2000 Plus refurbished only; its successor is the HomePower 2000 Plus v2 (see below).

The Quick Take: Power vs Portability

Both units hold about 2kWh, both use long-life LiFePO4 cells, and both expand with add-on batteries. The difference is what each one optimizes for.

Jackery Explorer 2000 Plus is the power pick. It runs 3,000W continuous (6,000W surge), takes up to 1,400W of solar, and scales to 12kWh with add-on Battery Packs. The trade-off is weight: about 61.5 lbs, which is why it rolls on wheels with a telescoping handle.

EcoFlow DELTA 2 Max is the lighter, easier-to-own pick. At 50 lbs it is roughly 11 lbs lighter, it has six AC outlets, 1,800W X-Stream wall charging, and it is available new at a lower price today.

Jackery wins the power categories (output, surge, solar input, expansion). EcoFlow wins on weight, outlet count, and current price and availability.

Output: Jackery Has More Headroom

The Explorer 2000 Plus delivers 3,000W continuous with a 6,000W surge, vs 2,400W continuous and 4,800W surge on the DELTA 2 Max (EcoFlow’s X-Boost can run some resistive devices rated up to 3,400W at reduced power).

That extra 600W matters when you stack loads: a microwave (about 1,100W) plus a window AC (about 600–900W) plus a fridge fits comfortably on the Jackery and is tight on the EcoFlow. For single appliances, both are plenty.

Winner: Jackery Explorer 2000 Plus — 25% more continuous output.

Solar Charging: Jackery Takes More Panels

The Jackery accepts up to 1,400W of solar across two inputs; the DELTA 2 Max takes 1,000W (two 500W inputs). Estimated refill times in strong sun, assuming panels deliver about 80% of their rating:

  • Jackery: 2,042Wh ÷ (1,400W × 0.8) ≈ 1.8–2 hours with a full 1,400W array
  • EcoFlow: 2,048Wh ÷ (1,000W × 0.8) ≈ 2.5 hours with a full 1,000W array (EcoFlow rates it at as fast as 2.3 hours)

In practice most people run 400–800W of portable panels, where both units recharge at the same speed. The Jackery’s higher ceiling matters if you plan a large fixed or ground-mounted array.

Winner: Jackery Explorer 2000 Plus — higher solar ceiling.

Portability: EcoFlow Is Lighter, Jackery Rolls

The DELTA 2 Max weighs 50 lbs (23 kg); the Explorer 2000 Plus weighs about 61.5 lbs (27.9 kg). EcoFlow is the easier lift into a truck bed or up a flight of stairs.

Jackery offsets its weight with built-in wheels and a telescoping handle, so on flat ground it is easy to roll from room to room. Neither is a carry-to-the-campsite unit.

Winner: EcoFlow DELTA 2 Max — about 11 lbs lighter.

Expandability: Jackery Goes Bigger

The Explorer 2000 Plus connects up to five Battery Pack 2000 Plus units for about 12kWh per station; Jackery lists the system at 2–24kWh when two stations are paired. The DELTA 2 Max supports two Smart Extra Batteries for 6,144Wh.

If you are building toward multi-day home backup, Jackery gives you a longer runway before you outgrow the platform.

Winner: Jackery Explorer 2000 Plus — about double the maximum capacity.

Wall Charging, Outlets and App

EcoFlow publishes a 1,800W X-Stream AC charging rate and supports AC + solar dual charging; Jackery also supports charging from AC and solar at the same time. Check each brand’s spec sheet for current charge-time figures, which vary by mode.

The DELTA 2 Max has six AC outlets; the Explorer 2000 Plus has five (four 20A plus one 25A). Both connect to a phone app over Wi-Fi or Bluetooth, and many owners rate EcoFlow’s app as the more polished of the two.

Edge: EcoFlow DELTA 2 Max — published 1,800W charge rate and one more outlet.

Lineup Update: Jackery HomePower 2000 Plus v2

Jackery’s U.S. store now lists the original Explorer 2000 Plus as refurbished. Its successor, the HomePower 2000 Plus v2, keeps the 2kWh class with some notable changes, according to Jackery:

  • 2,048Wh LiFePO4 capacity with 6,000-cycle cells
  • 2,400W output (lower than the original’s 3,000W, the same as the DELTA 2 Max)
  • 21 lbs lighter than the original 2000 Plus
  • Expandable to 12kWh with Battery Pack 2000 Plus v2
  • $1,199 list price; sold out on Jackery.com when we checked on Nov 10, 2026

If you want the original’s 3,000W output, the refurbished Explorer 2000 Plus is the way to get it from Jackery. If weight matters more, the v2 closes most of the gap with the DELTA 2 Max.

Who Should Buy the Jackery Explorer 2000 Plus

  • Home backup users who run several appliances at once and need 3,000W
  • Anyone planning a large solar array (up to 1,400W)
  • Buyers who want to grow toward 10kWh+ of storage
  • People moving it on flat floors, where the wheels make the weight manageable

Who Should Buy the EcoFlow DELTA 2 Max

  • Anyone who needs to lift it into a vehicle or carry it up stairs
  • Value shoppers — it is available new at a strong price right now
  • Buyers who want six AC outlets and fast wall charging
  • Most single-appliance outage, RV, and camping setups that stay under 2,400W

Our Bottom Line Verdict

For raw power and expansion, the Jackery Explorer 2000 Plus wins: 3,000W output, 1,400W solar input, and a 12kWh expansion path. Just know Jackery now sells it refurbished, and the new 2000 Plus v2 trades some output for a lighter build.

For most buyers today, the EcoFlow DELTA 2 Max is the easier recommendation: it is 11 lbs lighter, has six outlets and fast wall charging, and is available new at $1,029 (Nov 10, 2026).

Best for most buyers right now: EcoFlow DELTA 2 Max.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Jackery Explorer 2000 Plus or EcoFlow DELTA 2 Max better?

The Jackery Explorer 2000 Plus is more powerful: 3,000W continuous output (6,000W surge) vs 2,400W, up to 1,400W of solar input vs 1,000W, and expansion to 12kWh vs 6,144Wh. The EcoFlow DELTA 2 Max is about 11 lbs lighter (50 lbs vs 61.5 lbs), charges from the wall at up to 1,800W, and is easier to buy new right now. Pick Jackery for maximum output and expansion; pick EcoFlow for lighter weight and value.

How much does the Jackery Explorer 2000 Plus weigh?

The Jackery Explorer 2000 Plus weighs about 61.5 lbs (27.9 kg). It has wheels and a telescoping handle, so it rolls rather than being carried far. The EcoFlow DELTA 2 Max weighs 50 lbs (23 kg). Jackery says its successor, the HomePower 2000 Plus v2, is 21 lbs lighter than the original.

Which charges faster from solar?

The Jackery Explorer 2000 Plus accepts more solar: up to 1,400W across two inputs, vs 1,000W (two 500W inputs) on the DELTA 2 Max. As an estimate, a full 1,400W array delivering about 80% of its rating refills the Jackery in roughly 2 hours of strong sun; EcoFlow rates the DELTA 2 Max at as fast as 2.3 hours with 1,000W of solar.

Is the Jackery Explorer 2000 Plus discontinued?

As of November 10, 2026, Jackery's U.S. store lists the Explorer 2000 Plus as refurbished only. Its successor is the HomePower 2000 Plus v2 (2,048Wh, 2,400W output, 6,000-cycle battery, expandable to 12kWh), listed at $1,199 but sold out at the time we checked.

How much do they cost?

On November 10, 2026, EcoFlow listed the DELTA 2 Max at $1,029 (down from $1,299). Jackery listed the HomePower 2000 Plus v2 at $1,199 (sold out) and sells the original Explorer 2000 Plus refurbished. Prices change often, so check the current price through the links on this page.
