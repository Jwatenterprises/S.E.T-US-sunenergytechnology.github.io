The Mango Power E is not just a power station you charge at home and bring camping. It’s a power station you can also wire into your home electrical panel for 240V whole-house backup, expand with modular batteries up to 14 kWh, and run your central air conditioning during a power outage. No other portable power station does that.

This is where the Mango Power E stops being a “portable power station” and becomes a home energy storage system. The mSocket Pro accessory enables split-phase 240V output at 6,000W continuous — enough to run central air conditioning, electric dryers, well pumps, and other 240V appliances that no standard portable power station can touch.

You connect the mSocket Pro to your home’s main electrical panel through a transfer switch or generator interlock kit (installed by an electrician — typically $200–$500 for the install). Once connected, the Mango Power E powers your entire panel — every circuit, every outlet — just like a standby generator, but silent and indoors.

At $2,599 for 7 kWh + 240V + 6,000W, this competes directly with entry-level home battery systems like the Tesla Powerwall 3 ($8,400+ installed) and the Enphase IQ 5P ($6,000+ installed). The Mango Power system is less than half the cost of either, installs in minutes with a transfer switch rather than requiring a full electrical integration, and you can take it with you if you move.

Who Should Skip Mango Power

  • Ultralight camping and backpacking. At 100.1 lbs, the Mango Power E is a stationary unit with wheels. For camping, look at the BLUETTI AC200L (62 lbs) or ALLPOWERS R3500 (66 lbs).
  • UPS-critical equipment. The Mango Power E does not offer true UPS functionality. For servers, networking gear, or medical devices that can’t tolerate any switchover delay, the BLUETTI EP500Pro or a dedicated UPS is the right choice.
  • Buyers who want the most established brand ecosystem. EcoFlow has a deeper accessory lineup, more polished app, and wider retail presence. If you value brand maturity over raw specs, EcoFlow is the safer bet.
  • Outdoor-only use in rain. IP21 means the Mango Power E is not water-resistant. Keep it under cover. For outdoor-rated units, look at IP65+ options from Jackery.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Mango Power a good brand?

Yes. Mango Power is an engineering-led energy company using CATL LiFePO4 cells (the same supplier behind Tesla and BYD), with a 10-year warranty and 6,000-cycle rating. They design their own BMS, inverter, and app in-house. The Mango Power E has earned strong reviews from major outlets and holds a 4.3+ star average from verified buyers.

Mango Power E vs EcoFlow DELTA Pro — which should I buy?

The Mango Power E ($1,299) beats the EcoFlow DELTA Pro ($2,699) on price/Wh, cycle life (6,000 vs 3,500), max expansion (14 kWh vs 7.2 kWh), solar input (2,000W vs 1,600W), and warranty (10 vs 5 years). EcoFlow wins on AC output (3,600W vs 3,000W), app polish, and accessory depth. For pure value and expandability, Mango Power. For ecosystem and brand trust, EcoFlow.

Can the Mango Power E power a whole house?

The base unit handles essentials (fridge, lights, router, devices) for 8–12 hours. For true whole-home including HVAC, you need the 240V Power Kit ($2,599 / 7 kWh) or the 240V Pro ($4,199 / 14 kWh). The 240V Pro runs a 3-ton central AC for 4–6 hours and essential loads for 24–48 hours. Adding 800W of solar extends runtime indefinitely.

How long does the Mango Power E last?

CATL LiFePO4 cells are rated for 6,000 cycles to 80% capacity. At one cycle per day, that’s 16+ years. Under typical home backup use (partial cycles, occasional use), expect 20+ years. The 10-year warranty is the longest in the portable power station market. LiFePO4 chemistry is inherently safer and longer-lasting than NMC lithium-ion.

Is the Mango Power E worth it at $1,299?

At $1,299 (69% off MSRP), the Mango Power E delivers $0.37/Wh — the best value in LFP power stations. That’s cheaper than the BLUETTI AC200L ($0.49/Wh) and EcoFlow DELTA Pro ($0.75/Wh). The 10-year warranty, 6,000 cycles, 2,000W solar input, and expandability to 14 kWh make it one of the strongest deals in the market. The 100 lb weight is the main trade-off — plan on using it as a stationary unit rather than hauling it to campsites.
