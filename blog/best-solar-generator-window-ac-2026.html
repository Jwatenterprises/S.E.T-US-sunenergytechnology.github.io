Best Solar Generator to Run a Window AC Unit (2026):
Sizing, Startup Surge & Runtime

A window air conditioner is one of the hardest jobs you can give a portable power station. Here's how to size one correctly, what the startup surge means, and four units that can actually do it.

By S.E.T. Editorial Team Published November 11, 2026 🕑 10 min read

Quick Answer: Yes, a solar generator can run a window AC if its output covers the AC's running watts and its surge rating covers the compressor startup. For a 5,000–6,000 BTU window unit (about 450–600W), the EcoFlow DELTA 2 Max (2,048Wh, 2,400W, ~$1,029) is our top pick: roughly 3.5 hours of nonstop cooling, often more as the compressor cycles. For 8,000 BTU units or longer runtime, the ALLPOWERS R3500 (3,168Wh, 3,200W) gives the most hours per charge. Solar panels extend runtime, but rarely keep an AC running all day on their own.

How Many Watts Does a Window AC Use?

The size of an air conditioner is rated in BTUs, but your power station cares about watts. These are typical ranges for standard (non-inverter) window units while the compressor is running. Check your own unit's label: use the watts figure, or multiply the amps by 115.

The Startup Surge Is What Trips Most Power Stations

A fixed-speed compressor pulls a short burst of power when it starts, often two to three times its running watts for a fraction of a second. If that burst is bigger than the power station's surge rating, the inverter shuts off and the AC never starts. That's why a unit with 1,800W continuous output can still fail on an 8,000 BTU AC.

  • Inverter window ACs are much easier. They ramp the compressor up slowly, so the surge is small and partial-load power use is lower.
  • Leave headroom. Pick a power station whose surge rating is at least three times your AC's running watts if it's a fixed-speed model.
  • Plug the AC directly into the power station. Long or thin extension cords add voltage drop that makes startup harder.

How Long Will It Run? The Simple Formula

Runtime (hours) = battery Wh × 0.85 ÷ AC running watts. The 0.85 covers inverter losses. A 2,048Wh power station running a 500W AC nonstop gives 2,048 × 0.85 ÷ 500 ≈ 3.5 hours. Once the room is cool, the compressor cycles on and off, so real runtime is often 1.5 times longer or more. On very hot days it may barely cycle at all.

Can Solar Panels Keep a Window AC Running?

Solar helps, but it rarely carries an AC alone. A 400W array produces roughly 1.2 to 2 kWh on a good summer day depending on your location, while a 5,000 BTU AC running eight hours can use 2.5 to 4 kWh. The practical setup is: let the panels recharge the battery and cover part of the AC's draw at midday, then run the AC from the battery in the evening.

5 Ways to Get More Cooling per Charge

  • Choose an inverter window AC if you're buying new: smaller startup surge and lower power use.
  • Cool one small room and close the door, instead of trying to cool the whole house.
  • Pre-cool while the sun is up or while grid power is still on, then let the room coast.
  • Use the AC's eco or energy-saver mode, which lets the compressor cycle off.
  • Block the sun: closed blinds and a sealed window fit make a real difference to how often the compressor runs.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a solar generator run a window air conditioner?

Yes, if its continuous output is above the AC's running watts and its surge rating can handle the compressor's startup. A 5,000 to 6,000 BTU window AC usually draws about 450 to 600 watts while running, which most 2,000Wh-class power stations can handle. Runtime is limited by battery capacity: expect roughly 3 to 6 hours from a 2,000Wh unit, depending on how often the compressor cycles.

How many watts does a window AC use?

As a rough guide, 5,000 BTU models use about 450 to 550 watts, 8,000 BTU about 650 to 800 watts, and 10,000 to 12,000 BTU about 850 to 1,200 watts while the compressor runs. The exact number is on the unit's label: use the watts figure, or multiply amps by 115 volts.

How long will a power station run a window AC?

Estimate it with: battery Wh x 0.85 / AC running watts. The 0.85 accounts for inverter losses. A 2,048Wh unit running a 500W AC nonstop gives about 3.5 hours. Because the compressor cycles on and off once the room is cool, real-world runtime is often longer.

Can solar panels keep a window AC running all day?

Usually not on their own. A 400W solar array produces roughly 1.2 to 2 kWh on a good summer day, while a small window AC running 8 hours can use 2.5 to 4 kWh. Solar extends your runtime and recharges the battery, but for all-day cooling you will need a larger battery, more panels, or grid or generator charging.

Do I need an inverter window AC?

It helps a lot. Inverter window ACs ramp the compressor up gradually, so the startup surge is much smaller, and they use less power at partial load. That makes them far easier on a portable power station than older fixed-speed units.
