Best OSCAL Portable Power Stations 2026:
The Budget PowerMax That Undercuts Every Big Brand

OSCAL’s PowerMax lineup delivers LiFePO4 power stations from $429 to $1,699 — undercutting BLUETTI, EcoFlow, and Jackery by $120–$320 at every tier while matching 3,500+ cycle life and 5–7 year warranties. We ranked all 6 models.

By S.E.T. Editorial Team Published September 7, 2026 🕑 16 min read

Quick Answer: The best OSCAL power station for most people is the PowerMax 3600 (~$1,379) — 3600Wh LiFePO4 capacity, 3600W continuous output, drop-proof build, 5–8ms UPS backup, and expandable to 57,600Wh. For budget buyers, the PowerMax 1800 SE ($429) is the entry point — 1024Wh, 1800W, fast-charges to 80% in 45 minutes, and carries OSCAL’s longest 7-year warranty. Compared to BLUETTI AC200L ($1,299), EcoFlow DELTA 2 Max ($1,599), and Jackery 2000 Plus ($1,499), OSCAL undercuts by $120–$320 at every tier while matching LiFePO4 chemistry and 3,500+ cycle life.

Why OSCAL

OSCAL is the portable power station brand of Blackview, a Hong Kong-based technology company established in 2013. Blackview built its reputation making rugged smartphones — the kind used on construction sites and in extreme environments. They applied the same design philosophy to power stations: LiFePO4 chemistry, drop-proof enclosures, and aggressive pricing that undercuts established competitors.

The portable power station market in 2026 is dominated by four brands: BLUETTI, EcoFlow, Jackery, and ALLPOWERS. OSCAL is the challenger — less brand recognition, but pricing that forces a double-take. Their PowerMax lineup covers the full spectrum from grab-and-go portability to whole-home backup, all with LiFePO4 batteries and warranties between 5 and 7 years.

Three things differentiate OSCAL from the established brands:

  • $120–$320 cheaper at every tier. The PowerMax 3600 delivers 3600Wh for ~$1,379 — BLUETTI’s closest equivalent (AC200L) offers 2048Wh for $1,299. That’s 76% more capacity for $80 more. The PowerMax 1800 SE offers 1024Wh for $429 vs Jackery Explorer 1000 Plus at $499 with the same capacity.
  • Fastest UPS switchover in the industry. All PowerMax models switch to battery backup in 5–8ms. BLUETTI takes 20ms, Jackery takes 20ms, and EcoFlow takes 30ms. OSCAL claims their switchover is 50% faster than the industry average — critical for keeping computers, medical devices, and networking gear online during outages.
  • Maximum expandability. The PowerMax 3600, 3600 SE, and 6000 expand to 57,600Wh using BP3600 expansion packs. That’s over 7x what BLUETTI supports (8,192Wh) and nearly 10x EcoFlow’s DELTA 2 Max (6,144Wh). The PowerMax 2400 uses a cableless stacking system for tool-free expansion to 20,592Wh.

The trade-offs: OSCAL is newer in the US market. Brand recognition is lower than BLUETTI or EcoFlow. Their products are heavier at comparable capacities (98 lbs for the 3600 vs 62 lbs for BLUETTI AC200L). And their support infrastructure, while improving, doesn’t yet match the established players. If you value brand confidence and lighter weight over price and raw specs, look at BLUETTI or EcoFlow. For everyone else, OSCAL delivers more watt-hours per dollar than anyone.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is OSCAL a good brand for solar generators?

Yes. OSCAL is the power station division of Blackview, a Hong Kong tech company established in 2013 known for rugged smartphones. They use LiFePO4 chemistry across all PowerMax models with 3,500+ cycle life and 5–7 year warranties. The PowerMax 1800 SE has 4.5/5 on Amazon with 300+ reviews. They’re newer to the US market than BLUETTI or EcoFlow, but their specs and pricing are competitive or better.

OSCAL vs BLUETTI — which is better?

OSCAL wins on price and raw capacity (3600Wh vs 2048Wh for similar money) and UPS speed (5–8ms vs 20ms). BLUETTI wins on brand recognition, lighter weight (62 lbs vs 98 lbs), wider accessory ecosystem, and more established US support. For pure value per watt-hour, OSCAL delivers more. For the proven, lighter, more recognized option, BLUETTI.

Can OSCAL power stations be expanded?

Yes. The PowerMax 3600, 3600 SE, and 6000 expand to 57,600Wh with BP3600 battery packs. The PowerMax 2400 and 2400 Pro expand to 20,592Wh and 22,176Wh respectively using BP2400 packs via cableless stacking — no wires needed. The PowerMax 1800 SE does not support expansion batteries.

How fast do OSCAL power stations charge?

The PowerMax 1800 SE is the fastest: 0 to 80% in 45 minutes via AC wall charging with 800W input. The PowerMax 3600 reaches 80% in under 1 hour using dual AC + solar input simultaneously. All models support solar charging with 800W–2400W input depending on model. Eight different charging methods are supported across the lineup.

Does OSCAL have a UPS mode?

Yes. All PowerMax models include UPS (Uninterruptible Power Supply) functionality with 5–8ms switchover — the fastest in the portable power station market. BLUETTI switches in 20ms, EcoFlow in 30ms, and Jackery in 20ms. The 5–8ms switchover means connected devices stay powered during an outage with virtually no interruption, making OSCAL the best choice for computers, servers, medical devices, and networking equipment.
