Does the Anker SOLIX E10 qualify for the federal tax credit?

Yes. The E10 is a permanently installed home energy storage system and qualifies for the 30% federal Investment Tax Credit (ITC) when installed with or charged by solar panels. On a $3,999 system, that's roughly $1,200 back on your taxes. Consult a tax professional for your specific situation.

Can I use an Anker SOLIX power station indoors during a hurricane?

Yes. All Anker SOLIX portable power stations use LiFePO4 batteries and produce zero emissions. They are completely safe to operate inside your home, garage, or storm shelter. This is the #1 safety advantage over gas generators, which produce deadly carbon monoxide and must be placed outdoors.

How does the F3000 compare to the EcoFlow DELTA Pro?

The F3000 matches the DELTA Pro on capacity (3,072Wh vs 3,600Wh) and output (3,600W both). The F3000's advantages: 2,400W dual solar input (vs 1,600W), 7,200W surge (vs 7,200W), and pass-through charging at full speed. The DELTA Pro's advantages: slightly more capacity and a deeper ecosystem (Smart Home Panel 2). Both are excellent hurricane backup units. During this sale, price will likely decide it.
