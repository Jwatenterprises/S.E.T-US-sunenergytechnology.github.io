Frequently Asked Questions

How long do these sale prices last?

ALLPOWERS Fall Power Sale runs September 8–30, 2026. EcoFlow Autumn Sale pricing is available now as member-only deals. Prices and inventory can change. We update this page as deals shift.

Which solar generator is best for hurricane backup?

For most families, the EcoFlow DELTA 2 Max ($1,049 or $1,599 with solar panels) covers fridge, phones, router, and medical devices for 1–3 days per charge. For whole-home backup including AC, the DELTA Pro Ultra ($4,199) is the move. Read our Hurricane Season Solar Generator Guide.

Do these qualify for the federal tax credit?

The EcoFlow DELTA Pro Ultra qualifies for the 30% federal Investment Tax Credit (ITC) when used as a home energy storage system, bringing the effective price to ~$2,939. Portable units like the DELTA Pro and DELTA 2 Max generally do not qualify unless permanently installed. Consult a tax professional for your situation.

EcoFlow vs. ALLPOWERS — which brand is better?

EcoFlow wins on brand ecosystem (Smart Home Panel, app integration, wider accessory lineup) and resale value. ALLPOWERS wins on price-per-watt — the R3500 delivers similar specs to the DELTA Pro for $475 less. Both use LiFePO4 batteries with 3,000+ cycle lifespans. If you want the most power for the least money, ALLPOWERS. If you want the deepest smart home integration, EcoFlow.
