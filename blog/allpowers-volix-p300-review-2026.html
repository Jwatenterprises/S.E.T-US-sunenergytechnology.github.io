What You Get

The VOLIX P300 is built for one thing: reliable, affordable backup power that doesn’t compromise on battery chemistry. Here’s the breakdown:

  • 256Wh LiFePO4 battery — 2,500+ cycles to 80% capacity. That’s roughly 7 years of daily use before meaningful degradation. Most budget stations at this price use standard lithium-ion with 500–800 cycle ratings.
  • 300W continuous / 600W surge — pure sine wave AC output. Handles laptops, small fans, LED lights, phone chargers, CPAP machines, and portable coolers.
  • USB-C PD 100W — fast-charges MacBooks, iPads, and USB-C laptops. At this price point, most competitors cap at 60W or offer USB-C charging only.
  • 100W solar input — pair with a 100W folding panel for a full recharge in 3–4 hours of direct sunlight.
  • 6.4 lbs — lighter than a gallon of milk. This is a grab-and-go unit for day trips, car kits, and dorm rooms.
  • 6 output ports — 1x AC, 2x USB-A, 1x USB-C PD 100W, 1x 12V car port, 1x DC barrel.

What Can the P300 Actually Power?

At 256Wh and 300W, the P300 sits in the “small but useful” category. Here’s what to expect:

  • Smartphones: 60+ full charges (iPhone 15) or 40+ (Samsung Galaxy S24)
  • Laptops: 4–5 full charges (MacBook Air M3)
  • LED lanterns: 25+ hours of continuous light
  • Mini fan: 10–15 hours
  • CPAP machine: 1–2 nights (depending on pressure settings)
  • Drone batteries: 4–5 full charges (DJI Mini 4 Pro)
  • Portable cooler: 4–6 hours (40–60W models)
Won’t power: Hair dryers (1,200W+), microwaves (600W+), space heaters (1,500W), electric kettles (1,000W+), or window AC units. For those, you need a 1kWh+ station like the BLUETTI Elite 100 Mini.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the ALLPOWERS VOLIX P300 worth buying at $99?

Yes. At $0.39/Wh, it’s the best value LiFePO4 portable power station available in September 2026. You get 256Wh, 300W output, USB-C PD 100W, and 2,500+ cycle life — all for less than half the price of the EcoFlow RIVER 2 or Jackery Explorer 240 v2.

What can the ALLPOWERS VOLIX P300 power?

Phones (60+ charges), laptops (4–5 charges), LED lights (25+ hours), small fans, CPAP machines, drones, and portable coolers. It won’t run high-draw appliances like microwaves, hair dryers, or space heaters.

How long does the ALLPOWERS VOLIX P300 battery last?

The LiFePO4 battery is rated for 2,500+ charge cycles to 80% capacity — approximately 7 years of daily use. LiFePO4 is also the safest battery chemistry, with no thermal runaway risk.

How fast does the ALLPOWERS VOLIX P300 charge?

0–80% in about 1 hour via AC wall charging. Full charge takes roughly 1.5 hours. Solar charging with a 100W panel reaches full in 3–4 hours of direct sunlight.

How does the VOLIX P300 compare to the EcoFlow RIVER 2?

Nearly identical capacity (256Wh) and output (300W). The RIVER 2 is lighter (5.1 vs 6.4 lbs), charges faster, and has app control. But the P300 at $99 costs less than half the RIVER 2’s $209 price. If value is your top priority, the P300 wins. If you want the polish of app control and a lighter body, go RIVER 2.
