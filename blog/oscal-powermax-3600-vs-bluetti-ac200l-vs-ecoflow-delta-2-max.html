Why These Three?

These three units define the tier. They share the same battery chemistry (LiFePO4), similar port layouts, and a price tag that puts them head-to-head. But the differences are significant enough to matter.

Who Should Buy Which?

Buy the OSCAL PowerMax 3600 if: You want the most backup power for the least money. You’re comfortable with a newer brand. You need high solar input for off-grid use. You don’t need to carry it often (77 lbs). You want to run power-hungry devices (3,600W continuous, 7,200W surge).
Buy the BLUETTI AC200L if: Brand trust matters to you. You want the option to expand capacity over time with B300 batteries. You value the wireless charging pad. You’re investing in a system you’ll build on for years.
Buy the EcoFlow DELTA 2 Max if: You move your generator regularly (RV, van, between home and campsite). You want the fastest wall charging in the industry. Weight is a deciding factor. You want X-Boost to handle high-wattage devices beyond the rated 2,400W.
Don’t overspend on capacity you won’t use. If you only need backup for phones, a laptop, and lights (under 500Wh/day), all three of these units are overkill. Check our budget solar generator guide for units under $300 that cover lightweight use cases.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the OSCAL PowerMax 3600 worth buying over BLUETTI or EcoFlow?

On specs-per-dollar, yes. 3,600Wh for $699 (on sale) is unmatched. The tradeoff is brand maturity — BLUETTI and EcoFlow have larger communities and longer track records. If you prioritize raw value, OSCAL wins. If brand trust matters more, BLUETTI or EcoFlow are safer picks.

What is the best mid-range solar generator in 2026?

Best value: OSCAL PowerMax 3600 ($699, 3,600Wh). Best portability: EcoFlow DELTA 2 Max (51 lbs, 43-min fast charge). Best ecosystem: BLUETTI AC200L (widest expansion battery range, wireless charging). All three use LiFePO4 with 3,000+ cycle life.

How much solar panel wattage do these generators support?

OSCAL: 2,400W (highest — full recharge in under 2 hours). BLUETTI: 1,200W (~2.5–3 hours). EcoFlow: 1,000W (~3–4 hours). Higher solar input means faster off-grid recharging, which matters most during extended outages and remote camping.

Can these solar generators power a whole house?

Essential circuits, yes — fridge, lights, Wi-Fi, phone chargers, laptops. Not a full house with central HVAC. The OSCAL at 3,600Wh runs essentials for 24–36 hours. The BLUETTI and EcoFlow at 2,048Wh cover 14–20 hours. All are expandable for longer backup. For whole-home HVAC backup, look at the EcoFlow DELTA Pro or BLUETTI EP500Pro class.
