What You Get

The Elite 100 Mini packs a full kilowatt-hour into a form factor closer to a large lunchbox than a traditional power station. Here’s what stands out:

  • 1,004.8Wh LiFePO4 battery — 3,000+ cycles to 80% capacity. At one full cycle per day, that’s over 8 years of daily use before noticeable degradation.
  • 1,000W continuous / 2,000W surge — handles blenders, coffee makers, mini-fridges, power tools, and most small appliances. The 2,000W surge covers compressor startups.
  • 45-minute 0–80% AC charging — BLUETTI’s turbo charge technology (up to 1,100W input) makes this one of the fastest-charging 1kWh units available.
  • 200W solar input — pair with a BLUETTI PV200 for a full recharge in 5–6 hours of direct sunlight.
  • 10+ output ports — AC outlets, USB-A, USB-C (100W PD), 12V car port, wireless charging pad.
  • BLUETTI app control — Bluetooth + WiFi connectivity for remote monitoring, firmware updates, and custom charge settings.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does the BLUETTI Elite 100 Mini weigh?

23.6 lbs (10.7 kg) — the lightest 1kWh portable power station available as of September 2026. For reference, the EcoFlow DELTA 3 Plus weighs 30 lbs and the Jackery Explorer 1000 v2 weighs 24.2 lbs.

What battery type does the BLUETTI Elite 100 Mini use?

LiFePO4 (lithium iron phosphate), rated for 3,000+ charge cycles to 80% capacity. It’s the safest and longest-lasting battery chemistry in portable power stations — no thermal runaway risk, and a lifespan 5–10x longer than standard lithium-ion.

Can the BLUETTI Elite 100 Mini power a refrigerator?

Most mini-fridges and Energy Star full-size fridges, yes. The 1,000W continuous output handles fridges that run at 100–400W, and the 2,000W surge covers most compressor startups (800–1,200W). Fridges with startup surges above 2,000W may trip the overload protection.

How fast does the BLUETTI Elite 100 Mini charge?

0–80% in approximately 45 minutes via AC wall charging (up to 1,100W input). Full charge takes about 70 minutes. Solar charging supports up to 200W input, reaching full charge in 5–6 hours of direct sunlight with a compatible BLUETTI panel.

Is the BLUETTI Elite 100 Mini worth it over the EcoFlow DELTA 3 Plus?

It depends on your priorities. The Elite 100 Mini costs ~$190 less, weighs 6.4 lbs less, and has nearly identical capacity. The DELTA 3 Plus has higher output (1,800W vs 1,000W) and faster solar charging (400W vs 200W input). Pick the Elite 100 Mini for portability and price; pick the DELTA 3 Plus for higher-wattage appliances and faster off-grid solar recharging.
