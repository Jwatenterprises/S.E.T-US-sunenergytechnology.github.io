How We Ranked the ALLPOWERS Lineup

ALLPOWERS doesn't have the brand recognition of Jackery or the marketing machine behind EcoFlow. What it does have is a lineup of LiFePO4 portable power stations that consistently undercut the competition on price while matching or exceeding them on raw specs. That's the ALLPOWERS story in one sentence: more watts, more watt-hours, and more solar input per dollar than anyone else in the market right now.

We ranked the four current ALLPOWERS models — R3500, R1500, R600, and R2400 — using the same framework we apply to every brand. The five factors:

  • Value. What you get in capacity and output per dollar spent. This is where ALLPOWERS consistently separates itself from competitors, and it's a major reason the brand makes our recommended lists.
  • Performance. Real continuous output, surge handling for compressor-driven appliances, solar recharge speed, and UPS switchover time where applicable.
  • Expandability. Only the R3500 supports add-on battery packs. We weigh this heavily for buyers who might need to grow their system over time.
  • Battery chemistry & longevity. Every model uses LiFePO4 rated for 3,500+ cycles to 80% capacity. That's consistent across the entire lineup — no NMC holdovers.
  • Real-world use. Not "can it technically power X" but "will it comfortably power X for as long as you actually need it." We factor in weight, portability, outlet configuration, and noise.

The result is a ranking built around fit and value, not just raw specs. The R3500 has the biggest numbers, but it's also the only expandable model and offers the best dollar-per-watt-hour ratio in its class. How ALLPOWERS Compares to EcoFlow, BLUETTI, and Jackery

If you're reading this page, you've probably already looked at EcoFlow, Jackery, and the BLUETTI vs Jackery comparison. Here's how ALLPOWERS stacks up against the three other major brands, and where it wins, loses, and breaks even.

Where ALLPOWERS Wins

  • Price-to-specs ratio. This is ALLPOWERS' clearest advantage. The R3500 delivers 3,168Wh and 3,200W for ~$1,424 — you'd spend $1,800–$2,200 for comparable specs from EcoFlow (DELTA Pro) or BLUETTI (AC200MAX). The R1500 undercuts Jackery's Explorer 1000 Plus by $300 with roughly comparable specs. Across the lineup, ALLPOWERS consistently delivers more watts and watt-hours per dollar.
  • LiFePO4 across the board. Every current ALLPOWERS model uses LiFePO4 with 3,500+ cycle life. No NMC holdovers, no budget models with inferior chemistry.
  • Solar input. The R3500's 2,000W solar input and the R2400's 1,400W solar input are among the highest in their respective price classes. Fast solar recharging is a genuine competitive advantage for off-grid and extended-outage use cases.

Where ALLPOWERS Loses

  • App and ecosystem polish. EcoFlow's app is the industry benchmark for monitoring, remote control, and firmware updates. BLUETTI's app is solid. ALLPOWERS' software experience lags behind both — it's functional but not as refined or feature-rich.
  • Brand recognition and retail availability. Jackery and EcoFlow are sold at Home Depot, Costco, and Best Buy. ALLPOWERS is primarily online-only. That matters for warranty service, in-store comparison shopping, and consumer confidence.
  • Ecosystem depth. EcoFlow has smart home panels, dual-fuel generators, and modular battery systems. BLUETTI has the most flexible modular expansion. ALLPOWERS' ecosystem is narrower — the R3500 is expandable, but the add-on accessory catalog is thinner.
  • AC charging speed. EcoFlow's X-Stream charging can refill units from a wall outlet faster than ALLPOWERS. If you primarily recharge from the grid rather than solar, this matters.

The Bottom Line

ALLPOWERS is the spec-per-dollar leader. If you prioritize raw capacity, output, and solar input for the lowest price, ALLPOWERS wins. If you prioritize app ecosystem, retail availability, brand support, or modular expansion depth, EcoFlow or BLUETTI may be worth the premium. Are all ALLPOWERS solar generators LiFePO4?

Yes.
