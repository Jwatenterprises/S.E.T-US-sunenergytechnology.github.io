FAQ

Do I need Amazon Prime for these deals?

Yes — Prime Big Deal Days is exclusive to Prime members. A Prime membership costs $14.99/month or $139/year. If you don’t have one, you can start a 30-day free trial before the event and cancel after.

Should I wait for Black Friday instead?

In 2025, Prime Big Deal Days prices matched or beat Black Friday on most solar generators. If you see a deal you like during Prime Day October, take it. There’s no guarantee the same unit will be discounted again in November.

What about manufacturer sales?

EcoFlow, BLUETTI, and ALLPOWERS often run competing sales during Amazon events. We include links to both Amazon and manufacturer sites above so you can compare. Sometimes the manufacturer price is lower, especially with coupon codes.

How fast do the best deals sell out?

Lightning Deals typically run 4–6 hours. The best solar generator deals from Prime Day July 2025 sold out within 3 hours. If you see a price you like on this page, don’t wait.
